## Notifications
Fliks notifies you of what concerns you, on every device.

### Everywhere
- iOS and Android, even with the app closed, with the poster and action buttons such as Approve and Decline on a request.
- Browsers with the tab closed, and native notifications from the desktop app: turn on Keep running in the background to keep it in the tray.
- On TV, a short message while Fliks is open.

### Your choice
- Mute topics (requests, watch parties, social, new episodes, account) and devices under Notifications in your account.
- Plugins can notify you too, each with its own switch.

### Admin
- Mobile notifications go through the Fliks Relay, encrypted end to end. They are off until an admin turns on Fliks Relay active in Settings, Notifications.
